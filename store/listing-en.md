## Full description (max 4000 characters)

Haven is a recovery companion that stays with you on hard days and never judges you. You move at your own pace: a small step every day, and every return is a fresh start.

🌤 A FEW SECONDS A DAY
For each area you choose, mark the day as "I did it", "It was hard" or "I slipped". Whatever you pick, there is no judgment here. Ended streaks are never erased: your effort, your good days and your longest streak always stay with you.

🧭 AREAS THAT FIT YOU
Body-focused repetitive behaviors (such as hair, eyebrow or skin picking), obsessions, anxiety and panic, mood, eating and body image, behavioral addictions, substance use, self-harm, anger, trauma and stress, sleep. Choose as many as you like; each area has its own question, its own words of encouragement and its own calendar.

☁️ CALM
One tap when things get hard: a breathing exercise (in, hold, out), 5-4-3-2-1 grounding, bubble popping and a memory game. Support and emergency lines for your country are always one tap away. There are no ads on this screen.

🔔 GENTLE, PERSONAL NOTIFICATIONS
Every day around noon, a short quote that matches your areas, or a little hello with your nickname. If you have been away for a while, a warm note just to check in; never pressure, never blame. Notifications only arrive during the day, their content is hidden on the lock screen and you can turn them off at any time.

🤝 A SAFE COMMUNITY
Share experiences with people walking the same path, give support, send encouragement notes, add friends. Your real name and email are never shown; you join with a cute animal avatar and a nickname, and you can make any post fully anonymous. Posts are automatically checked for hurtful and triggering content. You can report anything that bothers you or block a person with one tap.

📓 MY NOTES AND MONTH LIGHTS
Write short notes to yourself. In the month calendar your good days light up, so you can see at a glance how far you have come. Badges, themes and small gifts keep you company along the way.

🎨 CHOOSE YOUR LOOK
Classic, Mist, Night Harbor, Garden, Cheerful, Notebook and the high-contrast, easy-to-read Clear: switch between seven designs in Profile with one tap. Fonts, colors, cards and the breathing animation all change together.

🌍 5 LANGUAGES
Türkçe, English, Español, 中文, हिन्दी. Community posts in other languages can be translated on your device, without sending them to the internet.

🔒 YOUR PRIVACY IS YOURS
Your data is never sold and is not used for ad targeting. Ads are off by default in sensitive areas. You can delete your account and all your data from inside the app in one step.

💬 SUPPORT AND FEEDBACK
Found a problem or have an idea? Write to the developer directly from Profile → Support & feedback and read the reply in the same place.

IMPORTANT: Haven is not a medical device, treatment or diagnostic tool, and it does not replace professional support. If you do not feel safe, please call your local emergency number right away.

Haven is designed for users aged 18 and over.
